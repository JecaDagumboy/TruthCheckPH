Before you press Share

  • Who wrote this, and can I find the original source?
  • When was it published? Could it be old or taken out of context?
  • Where else is this reported by reputable sources?
  • Evidence: Are links, documents, or named experts provided?
  • Emotion: Does it pressure me to panic, act fast, or forward it?

If you are unsure, pause and do not forward it.
